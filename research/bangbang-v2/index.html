The system

Bangbang V2 is a collaborative tele-operated avatar-robot project combining facial-expression and bilingual speech cues with a browser-based operator interface. Its AI outputs support an operator rather than deciding on the operator’s behalf.

My contribution

My work focused on the facial-perception pipeline and its Raspberry Pi 5 integration. I worked with a MediaPipe facial-landmark front end, a learned CNN/ONNX route and an interpretable action-unit route. The wider team’s system also includes speech and teleoperation components.

These routes offer different ways to describe visible facial movement. Their outputs are advisory cues, not validated measurements of a person’s internal emotional state or clinical condition. The human operator retains responsibility for the final decision.

What the public source contains

The public release contains facial-landmark and action-unit processing, emotion-model definitions, training/export scripts and Raspberry Pi vision examples. It also preserves an earlier stationary speech-and-vision prototype as a separate branch of work.

The early prototype uses Vosk with an English example vocabulary. It does not reproduce the complete bilingual speech or robot teleoperation system described in the team paper. The later vision and earlier speech branches are not presented as one merged application.

Datasets and trained model files are not bundled. Source inspection and compilation checks do not demonstrate a working camera, fresh device deployment or validated inference performance.
