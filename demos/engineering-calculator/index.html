The three relationships

Ohm’s law
I = V / R

Voltage in volts and resistance in ohms give current in amperes.

RC time constant
τ = R × C

Resistance in kΩ multiplied by capacitance in µF gives the time constant in milliseconds.

Voltage divider
Vout = Vin × Rbottom / (Rtop + Rbottom)

The output is measured across the bottom resistor. This ideal calculation assumes no output load.

These are ideal, single-step relationships. They do not model component tolerances, heating, capacitor leakage, loading or circuit dynamics.
