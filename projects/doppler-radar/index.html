EEEE2075 · Group 02 · 2025

Doppler radar speed detector

Yanjie Zhou · Siheng Ye · Xinyan Pan · Yitao Liu

A signal-chain project connecting an HB100 radar, analogue conditioning, STM32 frequency processing and a CPLD display.

From a weak signal to a display

The analogue front end amplifies and filters the radar output before ADC sampling. The STM32 estimates frequency using FFT processing, converts it to speed and sends formatted digits to the CPLD display. The integrated demonstration uses a 5 kHz sampling rate; the separate FFT exercise uses 10 kHz.

What the demonstrations show

The slides document a 2 kHz signal-generator exercise, a separate comparator-and-timer frequency counter, and a hand-motion radar demonstration. Noise thresholding and a low-frequency cutoff are used to stabilise the stationary display.

The stated 80 km/h range is a design objective, not a verified speed-accuracy result. Hand-motion examples are qualitative demonstrations rather than calibrated speed measurements.

Communication limitation: RS485 transfer remained one-way because the STM32 side lacked a transceiver. The slides identify a second transceiver and a PCB implementation as future improvements.
