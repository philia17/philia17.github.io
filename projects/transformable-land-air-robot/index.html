FURP 2025 · Team research prototype

Transformable land–air robot

Angrong Liu · Siheng Ye · Zican Huang

A FURP 2025 team adaptation of an open-source model shared on YouTube, combining ground locomotion and quadrotor flight.

Design basis and team adaptation

The prototype builds on an existing open-source model shared on YouTube. The team adapted the design and integrated the mechanical assembly, motion control and embedded systems for the FURP project.

The team’s Transformer Quadcopter poster describes two ground-drive motors, four propeller motors, two servos and two linear actuators. A Pixhawk 2.4.8 controller handles the flight system, while separate RC control supports the ground system and manual transformation.

My work within the team focused on mechanical modifications, motion control and embedded-system debugging. The prototype brought together structure, actuator travel, power distribution and control interfaces.

Prototype observations

The poster reports ground testing and low-altitude lift-off and landing. These are team-reported observations; the recovered material does not establish repeatable autonomous flight. Visual navigation, SLAM and adaptive autonomous mode switching are listed as future work.

Original record

Source: Transformer Quadcopter, FURP 2025 team poster v9. Supervisor: Dr Chiew-Foong Kwong; advisor: Fuhua Jia. The original author list is retained above. A final written report has not been recovered.
