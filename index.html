01

Robotics & perception

Robot–Elevator Interaction

Teaching a robot
to read the lift.

Recognising a button is one problem. Choosing the right one, locating it in 3D, and approaching it safely is another. This project connects those steps in a staged, guarded workflow.

RGB-D · OpenCV · calibration · robot arm

My contribution: staged perception, RGB-D localisation, calibration, and guarded arm integration within a collaborative research project.

Open the project notes +

The pipeline combines button detection, HOG+SVM classification, semantic target selection, RGB-D localisation, calibration, and guarded robot-arm interaction.

Evaluation: 0.9963 macro-F1 on 379 labelled crops; 85.1% offline request-level target selection accuracy. The two qualitative demonstrations are separate from evidence of generalised deployment.

IHCI 2026 · Paper 105 · accepted for oral presentation

02

Edge intelligence

Raspberry Pi 5 Engineering Assistant

A small machine.
A traceable answer.

A compact language model, course-material retrieval, and calculation tools. The aim: keep an answer close to the evidence that produced it.

Python · Qwen3-1.7B · RAG · Raspberry Pi 5

My contribution: quantised-model deployment, retrieval and calculation tools, the inspectable web workflow, and configuration comparisons.

Open the project notes +

A 4-bit Qwen3-1.7B deployment pairs retrieval with deterministic calculation tools. The web prototype separates generated answers, retrieved sources, and tool outputs.

Evaluation: seven configurations compared across 96 EEE questions in an offline GPU reference evaluation. Pi 5 CPU observations are reported separately, using the same quantised model artefact; reference scores are not Pi device results.

IHCI 2026 · Paper 106 · accepted for oral presentation

03

Human–robot interaction

Bangbang V2

Keeping the human
in the loop.

Facial and bilingual speech cues for an avatar robot. Developed the Raspberry Pi 5 vision path, while keeping the operator responsible for the final decision.

MediaPipe · ONNX · edge vision · teleoperation

My contribution: the facial-perception pipeline and Pi 5 vision integration within the collaborative teleoperation system.

Open the project notes +

The dual-Pi teleoperation system combines a MediaPipe FaceMesh front end with complementary CNN and interpretable FACS/AU routes, bilingual speech, and a browser interface.

AI outputs are advisory. The human operator retains final decision responsibility.

03 / A little context

Learning to see
the whole system.

葉嗣恒 / Siheng Ye

My starting point is electrical and electronic engineering. My next questions lie in robotics, control, and the intelligence we can put close to a machine.

I’m completing a BEng (Hons) at the University of Nottingham Ningbo China, with graduation expected in July 2027. I’m preparing for postgraduate study across embedded systems, control and automation, cyber-physical systems, and edge AI.

My current final-year project is a simulation-based study of digital-delay compensation and prediction robustness in PMSM predictive torque control. The work is exploratory; hardware validation remains ahead.

Across these projects, I care about what can be measured, what can be explained, and where a person should retain control.

Explorer of words & worlds. Outside engineering, I enjoy reading, learning languages and travelling.

I’m also looking for internships in embedded systems, robotics, control, electronics testing, and applied AI. During term, I can discuss 2–3 on-site visits per week near UNNC, with hours arranged around classes.
